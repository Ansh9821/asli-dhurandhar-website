About us

Flavor-packed comfort food, served with character.

Street Food ka Asli Dhurandhar is a beloved neighborhood snack bar in Sheikh Sarai, New Delhi. Known for its delicious grilled sandwiches, juicy burgers, and classic street-food energy, it brings together taste, affordability, and consistency for people who crave real flavor without compromise.
